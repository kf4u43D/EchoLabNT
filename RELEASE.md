# EchoLabNT 0.7.1-rc.5

Stereo delay for disting NT, focused on the colour and character of inserts in the feedback loop. Saturation, filtering, modulation and grain reshape the echoes on each pass, from warm repeats to rough, evolving textures.

20 treatments and 21 presets. Some demo presets can get a little wild; adjust them to taste.

**Firmware 1.18.0 / API 13 · Prerelease**

Download the ZIP below. With the NT powered off, copy `echolabnt.o` to `/programs/plug-ins/` on its MicroSD card. Back up and replace older copies, then restart.
